About this experiment

The field places arches and chooses image variations procedurally. New DeepDream panoramas form locally in the background while you keep moving. Three prepared AI landscape studies use the supplied marmot valley as a reference. Farther content is recycled to keep memory bounded; this is an endless drift illusion rather than an unlimited collection of unique landscapes.
