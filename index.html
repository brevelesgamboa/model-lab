About the models

What each model does and where to start.

Neural Growth

A cellular model grows textures across a grid. Try a pattern, then use the brushes to disturb it and watch it recover.

Open in LabRead the paper ↗

Inception Dream

Load an image and bring out patterns recognized by InceptionV3. Adjust the layer, detail, and focus before running a synthesis.

Open in LabHow it works ↗

DigiFace Generator

A decoder trained on synthetic faces generates new images. Explore the controls to see how features change together.

Open in LabModel details ↗

Chromatic Flow

A mathematical color field creates flowing patterns. Change the palette, scale, and motion to shape the result.

Open in Lab

Folded Fractal

Repeated folding, rotation, and inversion build geometric patterns. Small changes to scale and rotation can reveal very different shapes.
